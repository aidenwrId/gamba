Help Center & Wellbeing

Get answers to common questions about accounts, games, and support resources. Nebula Arcade is committed to transparent play and sustainable habits.

How do I earn credits?

New players start with 2,500 credits. Daily recharge bonuses and loyalty milestones add more. You can also trigger mock purchases to simulate topping up.

Is there real-money gambling?

No. Nebula Arcade never accepts deposits or offers withdrawals. Credits stay virtual and cannot be exchanged for money or prizes.

How fair are the games?

Every game uses secure random number generation. Slots follow a published payout table, Blackjack adheres to classic hit/stand rules, and virtual sports display the odds upfront.

Can I reset my password?

Yes. Visit the login screen, select "Need help?", and follow the emailed instructions. For security, we require verification before granting account access changes.

Where can I find wellbeing support?

We link to trusted organizations including the National Council on Problem Gambling and GamCare. Access these resources from your dashboard or by emailing care@nebulaarcade.io.

What data do you store?

We record usernames, emails, encrypted passwords, gameplay history, and loyalty progress. You can request deletion or export via privacy@nebulaarcade.io at any time.

Nebula Arcade promotes responsible entertainment. Take breaks, set limits, and contact support if play stops feeling fun.
