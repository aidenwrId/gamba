Terms & Responsible Play Charter

Nebula Arcade operates as a recreational platform that never converts virtual credits to real-world value. By accessing the site you confirm you are 18+ and agree to these policies.

User Accounts

Each player maintains a single account with accurate personal details. Sharing login credentials or creating duplicate profiles results in suspension. Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing.

Virtual Credits

Credits have no monetary value and cannot be redeemed, sold, or transferred. Mock purchases are purely illustrative and do not represent real payment processing.

Fair Gameplay

All game outcomes are determined by cryptographically secure random number generation. Payout tables and odds are displayed in-game for transparency. Exploiting vulnerabilities is prohibited.

Responsible Play

Nebula Arcade encourages session timers and regular breaks. Use in-app cooldowns or contact support for self-exclusion. We provide links to wellbeing organizations for additional support.

Data Protection

We safeguard personal data with TLS encryption and limit access to operational staff. You may request data deletion or export by contacting privacy@nebulaarcade.io.

Updates

Policies may evolve as new games and features launch. Continued use after updates constitutes acceptance; major changes are announced via email and dashboard messages.

Nebula Arcade is for entertainment only. If you experience negative play habits, pause your account and consult the resources in our FAQ.
