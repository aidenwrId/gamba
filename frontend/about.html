Crafting the Experience

Design Language

The Nebula palette blends deep cosmic violets with neon accents to evoke premium sci-fi lounges. We pair the bold Rajdhani font for headings with Rubik for friendly, legible text blocks.

Technology Stack

A FastAPI backend handles authentication, RNG game logic, and loyalty tracking. The responsive front-end is a lightweight vanilla JavaScript layer to keep load times fast on every device.

Player First

We celebrate responsible entertainment with optional cooldown reminders, transparent payout tables, and proactive resources for healthier play habits.

Nebula Arcade uses virtual credits only. Must be 18+ to access. Responsible play resources are available in the FAQ.
